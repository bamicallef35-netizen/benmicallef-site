    how the repositories relate

    • viab borrows the name of the building machine of I've heard about… (2005) with attribution, and the precedent of Olzweg (2006) as building by an open-ended stacking procedure. Its deposition rules follow TERMES (Werfel, Petersen and Nagpal, 2014). It does not model body chemistry or humours.
    • colloquy cites Une architecture des humeurs (2010) as a case of physiology treated as the input side of a design loop; its own loop is Pask's conversation, and it does not read or model physiology.
    • grey-walter-tortoise, offline-edge-node and umwelt-engine list Roche as reading at most, in one line each; their claims stand on Walter and on engineering constraints.
    • viable-system-model makes no Roche reference: Beer's management cybernetics has no documented connection to the practice, and any link would be invented.
